ABOUT CIVICLENS

Defending Public Infrastructure with AI

💥 The $42B Ghost Repair Crisis

City governments lose over 25% of annual maintenance budgets to ghost repairs — contractors billing for work never done, submitting old photos, or taking weeks to acknowledge critical citizen grievances.

Manual human inspections do not scale across thousands of square kilometers of roads, pipes, and electrical grids.

🚀 Our Mission

To eliminate corruption, ghost billing, and bureaucratic delay in municipal governance by deploying autonomous multimodal AI agents that inspect, verify, and enforce repairs in real time.

CivicLens believes citizen whistleblowers must be protected. We store zero personal identifying information (PII). No names, phone numbers, or user IDs are retained in our database — only verifiable civic evidence.
